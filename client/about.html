    Travel and Comfort in Urdaneta City

    Who are we?

    Founded in Urdaneta City, TBZ Car Rental was built on a simple premise: travel should be seamless, reliable, and comfortable. Whether you need a quick self-drive vehicle for a weekend errand, a professional chauffeur for a business trip, or a luxury SUV for a family vacation, we have the perfect ride waiting for you.

    But we didn't stop at transportation. We realized our clients were looking for complete getaway experiences. That is why we expanded to offer premium staycation packages, combining our top-tier fleet with exclusive access to fully-furnished executive condo suites.

    Our Mission:

    To provide top-tier mobility and premium accommodations, ensuring every journey with TBZ is safe, memorable, and hassle-free.
